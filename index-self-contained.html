Computational Drug Discovery: How Bioinformatics Is Reshaping the Pharma Pipeline

Our first community session explored how computational approaches, molecular docking, molecular dynamics, bioinformatics, and AI are shaping the modern drug-discovery pipeline.

Featured Speaker
Dr. Rajas M. Rao Assistant Professor, Yenepoya University, Mangalore
Special Thanks Dr. Siddhesh Uday Sapre — Scientific Co-Collaborator
View Key Takeaways →
✦
Session Summary

Key Takeaways

1. Power of Computational Approaches

Computational tools enable faster, cost-effective, and accurate prediction of drug candidates, significantly reducing time in early-stage drug discovery.

2. Molecular Docking

Molecular docking helps predict how a ligand binds to a target protein, a crucial step in identifying potential drug candidates and understanding binding interactions.

3. Role of Molecular Dynamics (MD) Simulations

MD simulations provide insights into the stability, flexibility, and behavior of protein–ligand complexes over time, offering a deeper understanding beyond static docking results.

4. Integration of Bioinformatics in the Pipeline

Bioinformatics integrates multiple datasets and computational methods to streamline target identification, validation, lead optimization, and ADMET prediction.

5. Impact of AI & Machine Learning

AI and ML are revolutionizing drug discovery by enabling prediction models, virtual screening, and de novo drug design with higher precision and efficiency.

6. Future Perspective

The synergy of computational tools, AI, and biological data will continue to transform pharmaceutical research and accelerate the journey from bench to bedside.
