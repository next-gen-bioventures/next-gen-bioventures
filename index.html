Meet the Founder

Deepa Mohan Poojary

MSc Biotechnology · Proteomics Researcher · Educator

Deepa built NextGen BioVentures from the ground up — as a researcher who felt the gap between biological training and computational tools firsthand. After completing her MSc in Biotechnology with a focus on proteomics, she set out to create the resource she wished she'd had: clear, practical, researcher-validated guidance for life scientists entering computation.

Today, NextGen BioVentures reaches 1k+ subscribers across four continents, with 18+ products designed to make bioinformatics genuinely accessible.
